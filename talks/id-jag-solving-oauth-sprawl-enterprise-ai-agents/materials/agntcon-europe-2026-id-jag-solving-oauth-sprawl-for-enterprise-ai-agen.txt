What the IdP misses
• SSO records the sign-in, not every downstream grant
• App permissions and tokens live in separate systems
• Disabling a work account doesn't automatically revoke every token

--- slide 7 ---
#AGNTCon #MCPConWhere the agentic stackis being built. 
How ID-JAG works
• Identity Assertion JWT Authorization Grant — an IETF OAuth draft
• The IdP approves the client acting for the user
• The app's authorization server decides what access to grant
• Allowed connections can proceed without another consent screen
