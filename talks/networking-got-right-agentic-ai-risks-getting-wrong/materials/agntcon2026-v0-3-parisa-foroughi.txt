Federating Agents Is Not Enough. Federate Domains.
An Agent Control Domain (ACD), as a first-class unit is the 
the largest entity that can make and enforce coherent assertions about authority, behavior/delegation 
and data.
Not a router.
An administrative and control boundary containing routers.
Declares what it will and will not transit.
The unit of inter-domain reasoning.
A G E N T  C O N T R O L  D O M A I N  ( A C D )
Not an agent.
An administrative and control boundary within which agents operate.
Declares permitted crossing classes.
The unit of cross-domain agent reasoning.
I N V A R I A N T  1
The ACD is the unit of inter-domain control not the agent. 
B E Y O N D  A  T O K E N  ·  keyed by domain, the crossing classes are few enough and slow enough to publish and converge. Keyed by agent or task, nothing ever converges.
I N V A R I A N T  2
The boundary requires modeling of crossing classes; the crossing classes will act as permission units as well as control 
enforcement capabilities that could be advertised.
