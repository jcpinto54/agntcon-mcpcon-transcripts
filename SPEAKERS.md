# For speakers

You spoke at **AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe 2026**.

Five rooms ran in parallel, so attendees only recorded the sessions they sat
in. This archive has transcripts for 19 of the 93 talks. The other 74 exist
here as a title, a time and a room.

## Adding to your session

Slides, the references behind the talk, a recording of your own, anything you
published afterwards — any of it can go in `talks/<slug>/materials.md`,
credited to you. Slides alone are a complete entry. A session does not need a
transcript to be worth something.

Sending one thing commits you to nothing else.

## If your talk is already here

An attendee recorded it from the audience, and it was transcribed with Whisper
large-v3, in almost every case on their own machine. Your name, company and
title were corrected against the conference programme, because speech
recognition is unkind to names. Nothing else was edited — no smoothing, no
tightening, no cuts.

Your audio is not published and never will be. No summary is layered over your
words: where one exists it is a separate file, labelled as derived, and your
words win over it.

## Corrections and removal

Say what is wrong and it gets fixed.

Ask for it to come down — the transcript, the summary, the background note, or
all of it — and it comes down. No reason needed, and the commit history goes
too, so it is not just hidden behind a dead link.
